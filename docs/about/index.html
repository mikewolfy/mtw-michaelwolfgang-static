About me

Husband, dad, runner, reader, and lifelong learner.

Hi, I’m Michael

I’m a husband and father, a Tae Kwon Do black belt and an avid runner. My best days are spent with my beautiful wife and wonderful kids, running outdoors, and enjoying a good movie or book.

I regularly listen to podcasts, read books, and try to stay curious about self improvement, personal finance and whatever I’m learning next.

Tae Kwon Do, as a family

Tae Kwon Do has been one of our family’s longest-running commitments. My kids and I have trained together for years, and every one of us has earned a black belt along the way.

Getting there wasn’t quick or easy — it took years of consistent practice, showing up on the hard days, and pushing through plateaus together. That shared effort is what made it worthwhile: we didn’t just get a belt, we built a habit of discipline and perseverance as a family, and we keep training together to this day.

Running

Twenty marathons, more than seventeen half marathons, a Boston qualifier, a thunderstorm run to the top of Pikes Peak — and I met my wife through running. You can find all of my races on Athlinks.

Work

By day I’m a software engineer and architect at CarMax, building applications and APIs with modern cloud technologies. If that’s your thing too, take a look at my work & tech corner.
